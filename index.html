Privacy Policy - Flip the Cards

Last updated: November 2026

Flip the Cards does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Data on your device. The game saves your progress (levels, stars and sound settings) only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or choosing "New Game" erases it.

Fonts. The game may load its text font from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your device's IP address as with any web font request. See Google's privacy policy.

No accounts, ads or purchases. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Children. The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact. Questions? Email: anchorgames53@gmail.com

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
